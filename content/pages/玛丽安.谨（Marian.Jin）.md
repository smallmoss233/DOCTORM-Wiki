## 概述

**玛丽安**是一位流浪商人，生成于**雪地生物群系**，在**特兰泽洛维度**刷新率**显著更高**。她行动缓慢，身体脆弱，却带着一副被宠坏的小孩脾气——而这个小女孩恰好掌握着**因果律本身**。

> 💀 **她很脆弱。但是她很记仇。而且她会去告诉她爸爸（DOCTOR M 的核心开发者）。**

> 💡 **一句话概括：** 一个拥有神力的小屁孩——而且脾气极差。

## 生成机制

玛丽安的生成由以下条件控制。

### 维度与概率

| 维度 | 生物群系 | 生成概率 |
|---|---|---|
| **主世界** | 雪地生物群系 | 8%。 |
| **主世界** | 其他生物群系 | 2%。 |
| **特兰泽洛** | 任意非海洋生物群系 | **30%**。 |

> 💡 雪地生物群系包括：雪原、雪林、雪坡、冰刺之地、冰封山峰、树林。

### 生成限制

- **不在海洋生成**——所有海洋生物群系均被排除。
- **必须在可站立的地面上**——使用 `MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES` 高度图。
- **每次生成 1 只**——权重 2，数量 1~1。

玛丽安**没有已知的出身**。她由一位养母抚养长大，对自己的来历毫无记忆。

- 对**因果律操纵**的精通——这让大多数时间领主都感到不安。

她最好的朋友是 **玛丽·谨**。

## 核心机制：因果与记仇

玛丽安的核心特征不是她的商品——而是她的**因果控制**和**铁打的记仇心**。她身体脆弱，但当她发怒时，**现实本身都会弯曲**。

### 记仇时间线

| 阶段 | 时长 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| **记忆窗口** | **2 个游戏日**（48,000 tick） | 她记住你的 UUID。在此期间任何进一步攻击都算作*持续侵略*。 |
| **愤怒状态** | **6 分钟**（7,200 tick） | 第一次被击中时触发。期间她**完全暴怒**：锁定你、拒绝交易、警告升级。 |
| **反击冷却** | 1 秒（20 tick） | 两次反击之间的最短间隔。 |

### 三振出局规则

玛丽安**正好给你三次**机会停止犯蠢：

| 攻击次数 | 她的反应 |
|---|---|
| **第 1 次** | 进入**愤怒状态**。播放一段 **Stage 1** 台词——委屈、受伤、警告你。 |
| **第 2 次** | 随机播放 **Stage 2** 台词——她真的生气了，威胁开始变得荒诞。 |
| **第 3 次** | 随机播放 **Stage 3** 台词——**随机的反击手段**。**没有第四次机会**。 |

> 从第 3 次起，每次命中都会触发反击——只要距离上次超过 1 秒冷却。

### “愤怒”意味着什么

玛丽安处于愤怒状态时：

- ❌ **她不会和你交易。** 无论你怎么右键，只会得到愤怒台词。
- ⚔️ **她会锁定你。** AI 状态切换到 `COMBAT`。
- 🧠 **她记得你。** 就算你跑了 10 分钟再回来，只要 2 天记忆窗口还没过，她就会接着上次的账继续算。

### 冷静下来

玛丽安只有在**连续 6 分钟没有受到来自你的伤害**后才会平静下来。平静后会：

- 清除愤怒标记。
- 重置侵略计数。
- 放弃目标。
- 回到 `IDLE` 状态。
- **可能**重新考虑交易（前提是记忆窗口已过期）。

> ⚠️ **注意：** 只要你还处于她的 2 天记忆窗口内，再次攻击她就会**从上次的计数继续**，而不是从 1 重新开始。

## Overview

**Marian** is a wandering trader who spawns in **snowy biomes**, with a **significantly higher** spawn rate in the **Trenzalore dimension**. She moves slowly and is physically fragile, yet carries the temper of a spoiled child — and that little girl happens to command **causality itself**.

> 💀 **She is fragile. But she holds grudges. And she will go tell her dad (DOCTOR M's core developer).**

> 💡 **In one sentence:** A little brat with divine power — and a terrible temper.

## Core Mechanic: Causality & Grudges

Marian's defining feature isn't her merchandise — it's her **causality control** and her **ironclad grudge-keeping**. She's physically fragile, but when she's enraged, **reality itself bends**.

### Grudge Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Memory window** | **2 in-game days** (48,000 ticks) | She remembers your UUID. Any further attacks during this window count as *continued aggression*. |
| **Anger state** | **6 minutes** (7,200 ticks) | Triggered on the first hit. During this she is **fully enraged**: locks onto you, refuses to trade, escalating warnings. |
| **Retaliation cooldown** | 1 second (20 ticks) | Minimum gap between two retaliations. |

### Three-Strikes Rule

Marian gives you **exactly three** chances to stop being an idiot:

| Hit | Her reaction |
|---|---|
| **1st** | Enters **anger state**. Plays a **Stage 1** line — aggrieved, hurt, warning you. |
| **2nd** | Plays a random **Stage 2** line — she's genuinely angry now, and the threats start getting absurd. |
| **3rd** | Plays a random **Stage 3** line — **a random retaliation method**. **There is no fourth chance**. |

> From the 3rd hit onward, every hit triggers a retaliation — as long as more than 1 second has passed since the last cooldown.

### What "Angry" Means

While Marian is in the anger state:

- ❌ **She won't trade with you.** No matter how much you right-click, you'll only get angry lines.
- ⚔️ **She locks onto you.** AI state switches to `COMBAT`.
- 🧠 **She remembers you.** Even if you run off for 10 minutes and come back, as long as the 2-day memory window hasn't expired, she'll continue from where the count left off.

### Calming Down

Marian only calms down after **6 consecutive minutes without taking damage from you**. Once calm, she will:

- Clear the anger flag.
- Reset the aggression counter.
- Drop her target.
- Return to `IDLE`.
- **Possibly** reconsider trading (provided the memory window has expired).

> ⚠️ **Note:** As long as you're still within her 2-day memory window, attacking her again **continues from the previous count**, rather than restarting from 1.

## Trivia

- **Not a TARDIS, not a human, not a Time Lord.** She's a humanoid paradox lifeform.
- **No known origin.** She was raised by an adoptive mother and has no memory of her birthplace.
- **Owns a Type-50 TARDIS.** It's her personal ride, mobile shop, and home.
- **She is the "developer's daughter".** Yes, really. She is the creator's daughter (OC setting), which is the **reason** she has causality powers and a bad temper — the "dad" she threatens to tell is the developer himself.
- **Mary Jin's friend.** Her closest companion.
- **Extremely slow and physically fragile** (speed 0.1). Her power comes entirely from paradox manipulation and grudge-keeping.
- **No safety net.** Unlike the Type-103 TARDIS, Marian's high-altitude throw **doesn't grant Slow Falling** — you'll fall straight down.
- **Two-day memory.** Even after she calms down 6 minutes later, she still remembers you for a full 2 in-game days. Attacking her within that window **continues from the previous count**.
- **Also spawns in non-snowy Overworld.** Just at only 2%, far below the 8% in snow.
- **Highest spawn rate in Trenzalore.** 30% — nearly 4 times the Overworld snowy rate.
